## Numerical conventions

- Model radar: five displayed methods, with min–max bounds from all 13 reported methods. Error metrics are inverted; R² retains its direction. A score of 1 identifies the best observed benchmark value.
- Error radar: raw row MAE on a shared 0–2.5 axis. Smaller radius means lower error. The complete values of both profiles also appear in the adjacent table.
- Improvement: unrounded paired exports give 10.73% for blend versus basic ExtraTrees and 8.54% for rich tree versus basic ExtraTrees.
- Temperature intervals compare exactly the same 1,118 observations. Pooled uses the clustered interval; within-pair uses the parent-bootstrap interval.
- All numerical graphics are native offline SVG. No dropdown, legend visibility toggle or modal substitutes another dataset. All 24 pages print together.
- Source hashes are embedded in `js/chart-data.js`, generated by `tools/build_chart_data.py`. Detailed report files remain unchanged.
